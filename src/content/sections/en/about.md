## Who is behind the site

I have multiple sclerosis.

When I was diagnosed, I did what everyone does — I searched the internet. I found a lot in English. I found very little in Bulgarian. And the little I found was either translated and cold, or frightening, or full of medical terms with no explanation of what they meant.

I wanted someone to tell me — in plain words, in Bulgarian — what MS is, what to expect, how the system works here. Nobody was doing that. So I did it myself.

## What MS Navigator is

An information site. Written by a person with MS — not a doctor.

The goal is to give you orientation: to know what to ask, what to expect, how to find your way. Not to replace your neurologist — no website can or should do that.

Every page ends with a reminder of this. It's not a formality — it's something I genuinely believe.

## What MS Navigator is not

It's not a forum. It's not medical advice. It doesn't sell anything.

The information here is gathered from publicly available medical sources, written in plain language, and filtered through my personal experience with the disease.
